1. Who we are

MY SHOP ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is a business ledger application built for shop owners to record and track sales, purchases, and expenses, and to optionally receive live customer orders. This policy explains, in detail, what information the App accesses, where it is stored, how it is used, who can see it, and how you stay in control of it at every step.

This policy applies to everyone who uses the App, regardless of whether you sign in with email, phone number, or a Google Account, and regardless of whether you choose to enable cloud sync or Drive backup.

2. Data you enter into the App

The core of the App is the business ledger you build yourself. This includes:

CategoryExamples
Sales recordsItem, amount, date, payment method, buyer notes
Purchase recordsSupplier, item, cost, date
Expense recordsCategory, amount, date, description
Shop profileBusiness/company name, logo image, currency preference
Orders (if Live Orders is used)Customer name/phone, ordered items, order status, timestamps

You control what you type into these fields. The App does not require you to enter anything beyond what's needed to record a transaction.

3. Signing in to your account

The App supports three ways to sign in, and you choose which one to use:

Email & password

Your email address and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text — this is handled by Google's Firebase Authentication infrastructure) are used to create and secure your account.

Phone number (SMS OTP)

If you sign in with your phone number, a one-time verification code is sent via SMS through Firebase Phone Authentication. We only receive confirmation that your number was verified — we do not store the SMS content itself.

Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, we receive your basic profile information (name, email address, profile photo) from Google to identify your account in the App. This is a separate, one-time permission from the Drive backup permission described in Section 5 — signing in with Google does not by itself grant any access to your Google Drive.

Device passkey / biometric unlock

If you enable fingerprint, Face ID, or a device PIN as a quick-unlock option, that credential is registered and verified entirely on your own device through your device's operating system — it is never transmitted to us or stored on our servers.

4. Cloud sync of your ledger (Firebase)

To let you access your ledger from more than one device, and to support the optional Live Orders feature, your shop's ledger data is synced to a cloud database (Firebase Realtime Database) that we operate as part of running the App.

How access is restricted

Each shop's data is stored under a record keyed to your unique account ID, and is protected by server-side Security Rules that only allow the signed-in owner of that account (matched by authentication ID) to read or write it. No other user of the App — including other shop owners — can query, browse, or access your shop's data through the App or the database directly.

Purpose

This sync exists purely so your ledger stays consistent if you use the App on more than one device, and so incoming customer orders can appear in real time on the Orders page.

5. Google Drive backup — how it works

If you tap "Connect Drive", the App requests one specific, narrow Google permission:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

This is Google's Application Data scope, and it is deliberately limited:

  • The App can only create and update one hidden backup file that it created itself.
  • This file lives in a special hidden area of your Drive that does not appear in your normal Drive file list, Drive search, or Drive mobile/web app — it cannot be browsed or opened directly by you or any other app.
  • The App has no access whatsoever to any other file, photo, or document already in your Google Drive, past or future.
  • No other app, including other apps you may have authorized, can read this backup file — it belongs exclusively to MY SHOP's connection with your account.
  • The backup counts against your own Google account's storage quota, and stays entirely within your own Google account — it is never copied to, or accessible from, any server we operate.

What gets backed up

A snapshot of the same ledger data described in Section 2 — sales, purchases, expenses, and your shop profile — saved as a single structured data file.

When it happens

Automatically a short while after you make a change (so backups stay current without excessive requests), and any time you tap the manual "Backup Now" option.

Restoring on a new device

When you reconnect Drive on a new device or after reinstalling, the App looks for the backup file it previously created (and only that file) to offer you the option to restore your data.

6. Local device storage

Independent of cloud sync and Drive backup, the App also keeps a working copy of your data in your browser's local storage on the device you're using, so the App works instantly and can function offline. You can also manually export a full backup as a `.json` file to your device at any time, and import it back in — this local export/import never leaves your device unless you choose to move the file yourself.

7. What we automatically collect

Standard technical information is generated any time you use a web application, such as your IP address and browser type, which may appear in infrastructure-level logs from our hosting provider (Firebase Hosting/Google Cloud) for security and reliability purposes. The App itself does not run third-party analytics or advertising trackers, and does not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

8. What we do NOT do

🚫 No reading your Drive files beyond our own backup file 🚫 No selling or sharing data with third parties 🚫 No advertising or ad trackers 🚫 No cross-shop or cross-user data access 🚫 No storing your password in plain text 🚫 No accessing your Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, or Photos

9. Google API Services User Data Policy

MY SHOP's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In practice, this means Drive data accessed through the drive.appdata scope is used solely to provide the backup-and-restore feature described in Section 5, is never used to train any AI/ML model, is never used for advertising, and is never read by human staff except as strictly necessary to respond to a support request you initiate.

10. Sharing & third parties

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The only outside parties involved in operating the App are the infrastructure providers that host it — Google Cloud / Firebase (authentication, database, and hosting) and, if you enable it, Google Drive (for your own backup). These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and confidentiality commitments; they do not use your data for their own purposes.

11. Data retention & deletion

  • Local data is stored on your device until you clear your browser/app data or use the App's own reset option.
  • Cloud-synced ledger data is retained for as long as your account remains active, and can be deleted by contacting us (Section 17) or through an in-App account-deletion option where available.
  • Drive backups can be removed at any time from your Google Account's Third-party apps & services page — this both revokes the App's access and deletes the associated app data folder.
  • Disconnecting Drive within the App stops future backups but does not, by itself, delete a backup already saved; use the Google Account permissions page above for full removal.

12. Security

All communication between the App and our servers, and between the App and Google's servers, is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Cloud-synced ledger data is protected by per-account Security Rules (Section 4). Google access tokens used for Drive backup are held only in memory during your active session and are never written to any server we operate. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

No system is 100% immune to risk, but the App is designed so that even in the event of a broader security issue, no single account's data is directly reachable from another account's session.

13. Your rights & choices

  • Access & export: You can export your full ledger as a `.json` file at any time from Settings → Data Backup.
  • Correction: You can edit or delete any sales, purchase, or expense entry directly in the App.
  • Deletion: You can disconnect Drive, sign out, clear local data, or request deletion of your cloud account data by contacting us.
  • Withdraw consent: You can revoke the App's Google permissions at any time from your Google Account settings, independent of this App.

14. International data transfer

Our infrastructure providers (Google Cloud / Firebase) may process and store data in data centers located in different countries as part of their standard global infrastructure. Your Google Drive backup, specifically, always remains within your own Google account and Google's own regional handling of your account — it is not moved to a separate location by us.

15. Children's privacy

MY SHOP is a business tool intended for shop owners and adult users managing a business, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country).
